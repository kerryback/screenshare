Trouble sharing?
  • Use Chrome or Edge. Safari on a Mac works too; iPhones and iPads cannot share a screen at all.
  • On a Mac, the first attempt may need Screen Recording permission for your browser in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • You choose what to send in your browser's picker — a whole screen, one window, or a single tab. Nothing is shared until you pick.
  • Your screen goes up only when the instructor puts it up.
